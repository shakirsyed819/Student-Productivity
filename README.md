# 📚 StudySpace — Student Productivity Website

**StudySpace** is a student productivity web application designed to help students organize their academic life in one place. It helps students manage their timetable, assignments, study sessions, notes, exams, and academic progress efficiently.

## ✨ Features

- 📅 **Class Timetable** — Organize and manage your daily class schedule.
- 📝 **Assignment Manager** — Add assignments, set deadlines, and track completed and pending tasks.
- 📖 **Study Planner** — Plan study sessions and organize your learning routine.
- 📌 **Notes Management** — Keep important academic notes organized.
- ⏳ **Exam Countdown** — Track upcoming examinations and prepare in advance.
- 📊 **Progress Tracker** — Monitor completed tasks, pending assignments, and study progress.
- 🔔 **Submission Reminders** — Keep track of upcoming assignment deadlines.
- 🎨 **Modern UI** — A clean, responsive interface designed for a better study experience.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of StudySpace is to provide students with a simple and organized platform to manage their academic responsibilities, improve time management, reduce missed deadlines, and develop better study habits.

## 💻 How to Use

1. Open StudySpace in your browser.
2. Add your class timetable and academic tasks.
3. Record assignments and their submission deadlines.
4. Plan study sessions and organize notes.
5. Add upcoming exam dates.
6. Track your progress and update tasks as you complete them.
